City Life and Events

The Sabarmati Riverfront is a major urban development project in Ahmedabad that transformed a polluted river into a public space with promenades, gardens, and recreational facilities.

Articles highlight its creation from a clean-up effort that involved removing slums and installing sewage treatment plants, and the project's aim to create public spaces alongside a mix of commercial and residential development.

It is now a popular attraction offering activities like boating and cycling, though some articles discuss criticisms regarding cultural impact and the process of relocating slum residents.

Despite the clean-up efforts, the river's water quality remains a concern due to ongoing pollution from untreated sewage.
